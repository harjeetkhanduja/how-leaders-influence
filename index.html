You might have these questions

Is influence the same as manipulation?

No. The difference lies in intent. Helping someone see things from your perspective is influence. Making people believe things that are not real is manipulation. The book shows the line through real examples.

I am not a boss. Is this book for me?

Yes. Influence matters most where you have no formal authority, with peers, partners, customers and even your own boss. And if you are a boss, every boss has a boss.

Can influence really be learned?

Yes. All of us influence every day, often without knowing the technique. The book structures what 25 leaders learned through experience into frameworks and habits you can practise.

How is this different from books on persuasion?

Most books on influence are written with a marketing mindset or for social media. This one is written for organisational leaders and combines credibility, relationships, alliances and context with Indian wisdom and modern research.
